What We Do

At Decora, we specialize in crafting unforgettable moments through bespoke event decor. Our services cover a wide range of occasions, including:

Church Weddings

Elegant and timeless decorations for your sacred ceremony.

Poruwa Ceremonies

Beautifully crafted traditional decor for your special day.

Beach Weddings

Stunning seaside setups for a romantic and breezy celebration.

Homecoming Events

Grand and welcoming decorations to celebrate your return.

Engagements

Chic and stylish decor to announce your special commitment.

Proposals

Create the perfect, memorable setting for your big question.

“Bride-to-be” Celebrations

Fun and festive decorations for the ultimate bridal party.

No matter where your inspiration comes from, we bring your vision to life with fully customized decorations. From woodwork and lighting to florals and personalized candle arrangements, every detail is carefully curated to suit your preferences.

We work with both fresh and artificial flowers and greenery to match your theme, and we offer specialty features like dynamic lighting effects and fireworks to make your celebration truly stand out.
