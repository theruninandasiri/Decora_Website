About Us

At Decora, we bring your special moments to life with beautifully curated event decorations and planning services. Our team specializes in a wide range of celebrations, including church weddings, poruwa weddings, beach weddings, homecoming events, engagements, proposals, and “bride-to-be” parties. With a passion for creativity and a commitment to excellence, we ensure every event is uniquely designed to reflect your vision and make lasting memories.

Our expertise also extends to a wide variety of other events, including birthday parties for all ages, holy communion celebrations, corporate events, Christmas gatherings, and New Year celebrations—both December 31st and the Sinhala and Tamil New Year. We also specialize in themed events tailored to your unique ideas.

What sets Decora apart is our commitment to custom-made designs. Whether it’s wood or steel structures, every element is built from scratch to perfectly match your vision. Our craftsmanship and attention to detail ensure you receive exactly what you imagined.

Since our launch in 2023, Decora has grown into a passionate team of 10 professionals, including specialists in woodwork, steelwork, and event decoration. We are proud to have served a variety of clients, including prestigious organizations like the Lion’s Club.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted event planning brand, known for creating extraordinary and personalized experiences that turn every celebration into a cherished lifelong memory.

Our Mission

To design and deliver exceptional events by transforming spaces with creativity, attention to detail, and a deep understanding of each client’s unique vision.
